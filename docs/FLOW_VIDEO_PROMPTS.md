# Google Flow prompts for the LinguaPal demo

The most convincing demo is a **screen recording of the real app** (see
[`demo.svg`](demo.svg) for the 6-scene storyboard). Use Google Flow / Veo only for
the small pieces it is good at: a **5–8s title bumper** and a **closing card**. Keep
one consistent style so the cuts feel intentional.

> Style lock (paste into every prompt): *"Flat vector motion-graphics, deep navy
> background (#0b1220), soft sky-blue glow (#38bdf8), single rounded character, subtle
> grain, no text artifacts, 24fps, 16:9."*

---

## 1 · Opening bumper (6s)
**Prompt**
> A friendly rounded glowing orb with two simple eyes and a small smile floats in
> from the right on a deep navy background, bouncing gently. Soft sky-blue light
> pulses behind it. Minimal floating language-bubble icons (speech bubble, flashcard,
> clock) drift past. Flat vector motion-graphics, #0b1220 background, #38bdf8 glow,
> subtle grain, 24fps, 16:9. Calm, welcoming, no text.

**On-screen caption (add in your editor):** `LinguaPal — a language tutor that runs on your own machine`

## 2 · Privacy / offline beat (5s)
**Prompt**
> The same glowing orb sits inside a soft laptop outline on a dark navy background.
> A friendly cloud symbol approaches, then gently floats away and dims; a small
> padlock icon glows calm green inside the laptop. Flat vector motion-graphics,
> #0b1220 background, #38bdf8 and #22c55e accents, subtle grain, 24fps, 16:9.

**Caption:** `Practice never leaves the machine — open weights, no cloud in the loop.`

## 3 · Spaced repetition beat (5s)
**Prompt**
> Three flashcard rectangles fan out and gently shuffle, then the front card flips to
> reveal a soft check mark. Small clock icon ticks in the corner. Flat vector
> motion-graphics, deep navy background, #38bdf8 and #a855f7 accents, 24fps, 16:9.

**Caption:** `New words become flashcards — and come back at the right time.`

## 4 · Closing card (6s)
**Prompt**
> The glowing orb settles in the lower-left of a deep navy frame as the empty
> center-right stays clean for text. Faint dotted grid, soft sky-blue glow.
> Flat vector motion-graphics, #0b1220 background, subtle grain, 24fps, 16:9.

**Caption (center-right):**
`Built for one friend · Open-weight Gemma · MIT licensed`
`github.com/surajns0033-collab/linguapal`

## Editing recipe (CapCut / Clipchamp / DaVinci — all free)
1. Scene 1 bumper (6s) → Screen-recording scenes 1–6 from [`demo.svg`](demo.svg).
2. Drop scene-2/3 motion beats as transitions if the recording feels static.
3. Narration or captions only — keep it under 90s.
4. Export 1080p, upload **unlisted to YouTube**, paste the link into
   `SUBMISSION.md` at the `[video demo link here]` line.

## If Flow is unavailable
A pure screen recording with captions is completely fine and judges accept it.
Flow is polish, not a requirement.
